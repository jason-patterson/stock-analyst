## 2. Company Overview

S&P Global Inc. is a diversified financial-information franchise operating five segments: **S&P Global Ratings** (credit ratings), **S&P Global Market Intelligence** (multi-asset data, analytics, and workflow tools), **S&P Dow Jones Indices** (the world's most-licensed equity benchmarks, including the S&P 500), **S&P Global Commodity Insights / Energy**, and **S&P Global Mobility** (automotive data, the former IHS Markit assets). The 2022 acquisition of IHS Markit transformed the company into a broader, more recurring-revenue data utility.

The business possesses one of the strongest competitive moats in the public markets. Ratings benefits from a regulatory-entrenched duopoly with Moody's and deep issuer relationships; Indices earns high-margin, asset-linked licensing fees that scale with global ETF and passive-investing growth; and Market Intelligence is embedded in customer workflows with high switching costs and ~70%+ recurring, subscription-based revenue. The result is exceptional profitability — operating margins above 44%, FCF margins north of 33%, and minimal capital intensity (capex < 2% of revenue).

Key risks center on the **cyclicality of the Ratings segment**, which is sensitive to debt-issuance volumes and rises/falls with the credit cycle and interest rates; regulatory scrutiny of the ratings oligopoly; competition in data/analytics from Bloomberg, FactSet, MSCI, and Moody's; and integration/execution as the company continues to digest IHS Markit and pursue AI-driven product extensions (e.g., Kensho, generative-AI tooling).

## 9. Investment Risks

**Bull Case (analyst target ~$534, +26% upside):**
- Sustained double-digit EPS growth from pricing power, index/ETF asset growth, and cross-sell of the combined IHS Markit data platform.
- A justified low WACC (~8–9%) given defensive, recurring cash flows — at 8.9% WACC and 11% growth the DCF supports ~$435+.
- Buybacks and AI-driven product extensions (Kensho, generative-AI analytics) expanding margins and the addressable market.

**Bear Case (DCF stress ~$275–320, −25% to −35% downside):**
- A credit-cycle downturn or rate shock that suppresses debt issuance and Ratings revenue (the most cyclical segment).
- Multiple de-rating from a premium ~27x trailing P/E toward historical norms if growth decelerates.
- Regulatory action against the ratings duopoly or competitive pressure in data/analytics compressing growth toward 5% (DCF → $275 at base WACC).

## 10. Verdict

**Recommendation: HOLD.** S&P Global is unambiguously one of the highest-quality businesses in the financial-information universe — a wide-moat, asset-light compounder with 44% operating margins, 33%+ FCF margins, and a deeply entrenched, recurring revenue base. The investment debate is therefore not about business quality but about price. My base-case two-stage DCF (11% phase-1 growth, 9.91% WACC, 2.5% terminal) produces an intrinsic value of **$365.68**, roughly 14% below the current $424 — signaling that the market already capitalizes much of SPGI's durability and growth.

The sensitivity grid shows the current price is defensible only under more generous assumptions: an ~8.9% WACC (reasonable for a stock with a 1.10 beta and defensive cash flows) or sustained 14%+ FCF growth. Because both are plausible for a franchise of this caliber, I do not view the shares as a SELL; rather, the risk/reward at $424 is balanced. The 21-analyst consensus of $533.76 (Strong Buy) reflects a more bullish growth/discount-rate stance, and even the low target ($480) exceeds my fair value — a gap investors should weigh against the limited margin of safety in a disciplined DCF.

**Fair value range: $366 (base DCF) to $435 (quality-adjusted, lower WACC).** I would be a more aggressive buyer on weakness toward the low-$380s (near the 52-week low and offering a clearer discount to intrinsic value). **Catalysts to watch:** the trajectory of global debt issuance/credit conditions (Ratings), net new subscription growth and price realization in Market Intelligence, ETF/index asset inflows, and AI-product monetization. **Time horizon: 12–24 months.**
